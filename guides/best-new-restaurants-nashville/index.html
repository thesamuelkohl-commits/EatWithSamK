⭐ Thai Samurai

Neighborhood: River North (East Bank)

Sam Score: 9.0/10

Best For: Thai and sushi with a show

Price: $$$

Series: Trying Nashville's Newest Restaurants, Pt. 1

Thai Samurai serves Thai food and sushi on the East Bank in River North. There's a tuk-tuk parked out front, the bar sits under big backlit samurai murals, and the Flaming Tiger Roll comes out wrapped in foil and gets set on fire at the table.

I got a Thai Spicy Margarita, the Volcanic Lobster, Drunken Noodles with chicken at level 3 hot, and the Flaming Tiger Roll. It's the highest score I've given a new opening so far. It's in a cool part of town, and service was slow. Read Sam's full review →

⭐ Halal Birdz

Neighborhood: South Nashville (Murfreesboro Pike)

Sam Score: 8.9/10

Best For: Halal hot chicken and snack packs

Price: $$

Series: Trying Nashville's Newest Restaurants

Halal Birdz is a newer halal hot chicken counter on Murfreesboro Pike, serving Nashville-style tenders, wings and loaded snack packs.

I got the #3 Broadway Box and the #4 Halal Snack Pack, both at medium heat. Service was quick and the presentation is a big part of it. The shake machine was down and there isn't much seating. Read Sam's full review →

⭐ Chen Chen's

Neighborhood: Midtown

Sam Score: 8.6/10

Best For: Hot chicken with Asian-inspired sauces

Price: $$

Series: Trying Nashville's Newest Restaurants

Chen Chen's started as a Toronto pop-up from chef Chen Chen, who was born in China and raised in Nashville. After being named Yelp Canada's #1 Place to Eat in 2024, he's brought it back to his hometown, pairing Nashville hot chicken with his own Asian-inspired sauces.

I got the homemade mac and cheese with hot chicken bites in Szechuan sauce at medium heat, plus waffle fries. Quick, friendly, and short on indoor seating. Read Sam's full review →

⭐ Personally Reviewed by Eat With Sam K · Read my full Chen Chen's review →

⭐ NADC Burger

Neighborhood: Downtown (The Arcade)

Sam Score: 8.2/10

Best For: One Wagyu smash burger, done one way

Price: $$

Series: Trying Nashville's Newest Restaurants

NADC, short for "Not A Damn Chance," is a single-item burger counter inside the historic Arcade downtown. There's one burger: two smashed 3 oz Wagyu patties, American cheese, secret sauce, onions, pickles and jalapeños on a griddled bun.

I got it with everything, plus fries and the secret sauce. It's quick and it's in a cool part of town, with no seating inside. Read Sam's full review →

What Counts as a "New" Nashville Restaurant?

For this guide I'm focused on restaurants that recently opened, or that are new enough that people are still deciding whether they're worth visiting.

Restaurants don't stay in the "new" category forever. As Nashville keeps opening places, older ones move into my other guides and rankings instead, which keeps this page focused on what's actually new right now.

How I Rank Nashville's New Restaurants

A restaurant doesn't get ranked highly just because it's new or getting attention. I'm looking at the whole experience:

  • 🍴 Food quality: does the food actually deliver?
  • 💰 Value: does the experience justify what you're paying?
  • 👀 What stands out: is there a reason to pick it over dozens of other Nashville restaurants?
  • 🏠 Atmosphere: does it deliver the experience it's going for?
  • 🔄 Would I go back: would I spend my own money to return?

The Sam Score is my overall take on all of it.
