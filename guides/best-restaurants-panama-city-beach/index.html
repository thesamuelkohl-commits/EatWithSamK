⭐ Sharky's Beachfront Restaurant

Neighborhood: Front Beach Road, on the Gulf

Sam Score: 7.9/10

Best For: Families, big groups and eating with a view

Price: $$

Sharky's is a Panama City Beach institution sitting right on the sand, a big open-air place with a tiki bar and tables looking straight out at the Gulf.

The view is the headline, and the seating and service keep up with it: plenty of tables and food out fast even when it's busy. Worth knowing that it's mostly open-air, so the weather is part of the experience. Read Sam's full review →

🦪 Dusty's Oyster Bar

If you're searching for seafood in Panama City Beach, there's a good chance you'll hear about Dusty's Oyster Bar. Dusty's has the kind of atmosphere you want from an old-school Florida oyster bar. It's casual, busy, and much more about grabbing seafood and having a good time than getting a polished fine-dining experience.

Best For: Oysters, seafood and a casual local atmosphere

Price: $$

📍 I've been here. My full review and Sam Score are coming soon, and this entry moves to the top of the guide once it's live.

🍺 McGuire's Irish Pub

McGuire's brought its well-known Irish pub concept to Panama City Beach and offers a completely different experience from the seafood-heavy restaurants you'll find throughout PCB. It's one I'm interested in trying as I continue exploring the area.

Best For: Groups, pub food, drinks and a lively atmosphere

🐟 Mike's Cafe & Oyster Bar

If you're looking for another casual seafood and oyster option, Mike's Cafe & Oyster Bar is one of the local names worth knowing. The menu leans heavily into the kind of seafood you'd expect on a Florida beach trip, including oysters and other Gulf Coast favorites.

Best For: Casual seafood and oysters

🦐 Local Steamer Seafood Market

Local Steamer is particularly interesting if your priority is the seafood itself rather than a traditional sit-down restaurant experience. Seafood boils and Gulf Coast favorites make this a spot I'd consider for anyone wanting a more casual PCB seafood meal.

Best For: Seafood boils, shellfish and casual takeout

Photo via their website, not personally taken

🍽️ Hunt & Gather Raw Bar & Pub

For something a little more chef-driven, Hunt & Gather is one of the Panama City Beach restaurants on my list to try. It offers a different experience from many of PCB's large beachfront and tourist-oriented restaurants, making it especially interesting if you're looking for a more food-focused dinner.

Best For: Date night and a more elevated meal

✨ Firefly

Firefly is another option to consider when you're looking for a nicer dinner in Panama City Beach. Rather than the ultra-casual beach atmosphere you'll find at many PCB restaurants, Firefly is geared more toward an elevated night out.

Best For: Date night, celebrations and nicer dinners

What Food Is Panama City Beach Known For?

Seafood is the obvious answer. Being on Florida's Gulf Coast means you'll find plenty of oysters, shrimp, fish, crab and seafood boils throughout Panama City Beach.

But that's also why I wouldn't spend an entire PCB trip eating at restaurants simply because they're on the water. I'm looking for the places where the food itself is worth the visit, whether that's a seafood shack, oyster bar, burger joint, breakfast restaurant or an upscale dinner.

Where Should You Eat in Panama City Beach?

If it's your first trip, I'd mix up your restaurant choices rather than eating at the same type of place every night.

Try an old-school seafood or oyster bar, have at least one nicer dinner, and leave room for some of the less obvious restaurants away from the biggest tourist areas.
