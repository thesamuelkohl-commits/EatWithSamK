Best Mexican Restaurants in Nashville (2026): Where to Eat

Nashville, TNSeptember 21, 2026🔄 Last updated: September 2026

Nashville might be known for hot chicken, but the city has a Mexican food scene worth exploring too.

From street tacos and birria to massive burritos, traditional dishes and more modern Mexican restaurants, there are plenty of options around Nashville.

I'm eating my way through the city to figure out which restaurants are actually worth your money. When I personally review a restaurant, I pay for my own meal and give it an independent Sam Score out of 10.

This guide will continue to grow as I try more Mexican restaurants across Nashville.

⭐ Chilangos Tacos

Neighborhood: Downtown (Assembly Food Hall)

Sam Score: 8.7/10

Best For: Tacos near Broadway

Price: $$

Chilangos is a taco counter on Level 2 North of Assembly Food Hall at Fifth + Broadway, the Nashville outpost of a Dallas-based taco brand. The menu is tacos, quesadillas and nachos with classic and specialty fillings.

I got a chicken, a steak and a pastor taco, plus chips and queso. Service was quick and there's plenty of seating, though the food hall gets crowded and parking downtown is paid. Read Sam's full review →

⭐ The Chile Burrito Co.

Neighborhood: Downtown, just off Broadway

Sam Score: 8.7/10

Best For: Build-your-own burritos

Price: $$

The Chile Burrito Co. is a counter-service Mexican spot a block off Broadway, with build-your-own burritos, tacos and a salsa bar in a colorful dining room.

I got a 12-inch steak burrito with rice, cheese, queso, pico, cilantro, onions, lettuce and medium sauce, plus chips. It's quick and close to Broadway. The trade-offs are limited seating, paid parking only, and hours worth checking before you go. Read Sam's full review →

⭐ Ladybird Taco

Neighborhood: 12 South

Sam Score: 7.6/10

Best For: Breakfast tacos

Price: $$

Ladybird is a Texas-style breakfast taco counter in 12 South that calls itself "a little TX in TN," so it leans Tex-Mex rather than traditional Mexican. Expect a numbered menu of breakfast tacos, coffee and lunch options at communal tables.

I got breakfast tacos #1, #4 and #6. It's quick and parking nearby is free, but it gets packed and you may wait for a table. Read Sam's full review →

More Nashville Mexican Restaurants Worth Knowing

These haven't been Sam Scored yet. Rather than ranking places I haven't personally reviewed, I'm keeping them on my radar and will add them to the rankings once I've eaten there.

🌯 Baja Burrito

An independent burrito shop in Berry Hill that's been open since 2000, well before most of Nashville's current Mexican spots. It's the classic casual burrito-and-tacos stop in that part of town.

Best For: Burritos, casual lunch

🔥 Mas Tacos Por Favor

A long-running East Nashville taco spot and one of the best-known casual Mexican names in the city, serving tacos alongside soups and sides.

Best For: Nashville taco institution, affordable

🧀 El Jaliciense

A family-run restaurant on Gallatin Pike in East Nashville since 2006, cooking home-style food from Jalisco. It's the one to look at for a traditional sit-down Mexican meal, and birria is one of the dishes it's known for.

Best For: Traditional Mexican, birria

🌮 Taqueria San Luis

A no-frills taqueria for street tacos. Order a few different meats rather than a plate of one, so you can find your favorite.

Best For: Street tacos, value

🌽 Maiz de la Vida

Built around fresh masa and handmade tortillas, which makes the tortilla the point of the meal rather than an afterthought.

Best For: Handmade tortillas

🇲🇽 Superica

Tex-Mex rather than traditional Mexican, with tacos, fajitas, queso and margaritas in a polished sit-down room. I've already reviewed the Atlanta location at The Battery and gave it an 8.1, so the Nashville one is on my list to compare.

Best For: Tex-Mex, margaritas, sit-down meals

Traditional Mexican Food Worth Trying

I'm not going to crown anything the "most authentic" Mexican food in Nashville. Mexican cooking is regional, and what's traditional in Jalisco isn't the same as what's traditional somewhere else. What's more useful is knowing what a restaurant focuses on.

El Jaliciense cooks home-style dishes from Jalisco, Maiz de la Vida is built around fresh masa, and the taquerias along Nolensville Pike in South Nashville are some of the best places in the city to explore street tacos, markets and regional dishes.

What Should You Order at a Mexican Restaurant?

If you're trying somewhere new, these are the dishes I typically look for:

  • 🌮 Tacos: great for comparing meats, tortillas, toppings and salsa.
  • 🧀 Birria: especially when it's served with consommé.
  • 🌯 Burritos: a good test of portions, ingredients and overall value.
  • 🫓 Quesadillas: simple, but surprisingly easy to get wrong.
  • ⭐ House specialties: usually more interesting than ordering the same thing everywhere.

And when a restaurant is known for something specific, that's usually what I'm ordering for the review.

Where to Find Mexican Food in Nashville

Don't limit your search to downtown. Two of my highest rated spots are near Broadway, but some of Nashville's most interesting Mexican food is spread across the city:

  • 📍 Downtown: Chilangos Tacos and The Chile Burrito Co.
  • 🏘️ 12 South: Ladybird Taco
  • 🎸 East Nashville: Mas Tacos Por Favor, and El Jaliciense on Gallatin Pike
  • 🌯 Berry Hill: Baja Burrito
  • 🌶️ Nolensville Pike: taquerias, markets and food trucks throughout South Nashville
