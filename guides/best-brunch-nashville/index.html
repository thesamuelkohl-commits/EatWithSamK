Best Brunch in Nashville (2026 Updated Guide)

Nashville, TNAugust 3, 2026

Searching for the best brunch in Nashville? Whether you're craving chicken and waffles, avocado toast, giant cinnamon rolls, bottomless mimosas, or the perfect breakfast burrito, this guide highlights some of the best brunch spots Music City has to offer.

Brunch has become one of Nashville's biggest dining traditions. From neighborhood cafés and trendy patios to upscale brunch destinations, there's no shortage of incredible places to spend a Saturday or Sunday morning.

At Eat With Sam K, every meal is purchased with my own money. I don't accept paid reviews or exchange positive ratings for free food. I'm working my way through Nashville's brunch scene and giving the places I personally visit a Sam Score based on my overall experience.

Tip: hover over any $ price tag below to see what it means.

⭐ Biscuit Love

Neighborhood: The Gulch

Sam Score: 7.4/10

Best For: First-time Nashville visitors

Price: $$

Biscuit Love is one of Nashville's most recognizable brunch spots, a Gulch mainstay known for scratch-made biscuits and Southern breakfast classics.

Good environment and a great patio make the wait worth it, though it can get busy and there's often a line to get in. Read Sam's full review →

💬 Sam Says: "A great area and a great patio, just budget extra time for the wait."

More Nashville Brunch Worth Knowing

I'm still working my way through Nashville's brunch scene, so the restaurants below haven't received an official Sam Score yet.

Rather than assigning rankings to places I haven't personally reviewed, I'm keeping them on my radar and will update this guide as I visit them.

🍯 Milk & Honey

Milk & Honey has become one of the city's most popular brunch destinations thanks to its beautiful atmosphere, creative menu, and consistently excellent breakfast dishes. Whether you're meeting friends or grabbing brunch before exploring Nashville, this is one of the safest recommendations in town.

Best For: Classic Nashville Brunch

🧈 The Butter Milk Ranch

Known for generous portions and Southern-inspired dishes, The Butter Milk Ranch has become a favorite among locals looking for hearty brunch food. The biscuits alone are worth the visit.

Best For: Southern Comfort Brunch

🥞 Snooze, an A.M. Eatery

Snooze offers one of Nashville's most creative brunch menus, featuring unique pancakes, breakfast tacos, Benedicts, and cocktails, perfect for groups looking for plenty of variety.

Best For: Creative Breakfast

🥂 Adele's

If you're looking for a more elevated brunch experience, Adele's continues to be one of Nashville's most popular choices. Their weekend brunch buffet has earned a strong reputation among locals.

Best For: Upscale Weekend Brunch

🇫🇷 Liberty Common

Located downtown, Liberty Common combines French café vibes with classic brunch favorites, an excellent choice before exploring Broadway or attending nearby events.

Best For: French-Inspired Brunch

☕ Hearts

Hearts is one of Nashville's newer favorites, offering quality coffee alongside fresh pastries and lighter breakfast options, great for casual mornings or working remotely for a few hours.

Best For: Coffee & Light Brunch

🏘️ Fenwick's 300

Fenwick's has built a loyal following by serving reliable breakfast favorites without the tourist crowds, a great option if you want a more relaxed local brunch experience.

Best For: Local Neighborhood Brunch

🍳 Another Broken Egg Cafe

With an extensive breakfast and brunch menu, Another Broken Egg is a dependable choice for groups with different tastes, everything from omelets and pancakes to seafood-inspired brunch dishes.

Best For: Variety

🦞 The Hampton Social

Known for its bright coastal atmosphere and fun brunch cocktails, The Hampton Social is one of Nashville's most Instagram-worthy brunch destinations, ideal for celebrations, birthdays, and larger groups.

Best For: Brunch with Friends

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best brunch in Nashville?

Of the brunch spots Sam has personally reviewed so far, Biscuit Love is his highest rated at 7.4/10. Milk & Honey, The Butter Milk Ranch, Snooze, and Adele's are also consistently among Nashville's most popular brunch destinations, and I'll keep adding my own rankings as I visit more of them.

Where should tourists go for brunch in Nashville?

Biscuit Love and Milk & Honey are two of the city's most iconic brunch spots and are great choices for first-time visitors. I've personally reviewed Biscuit Love, so you can see exactly what I ordered and how I rated it.

Which Nashville brunch has the shortest wait?

Neighborhood restaurants like Fenwick's 300 or Hearts often have shorter waits than Nashville's biggest brunch destinations.

What is the best brunch for a date?

Milk & Honey, Adele's, and Liberty Common all offer excellent atmospheres for brunch dates.

What is the best brunch for groups?

The Hampton Social, Snooze, and Another Broken Egg Cafe all work well for larger groups thanks to spacious seating and diverse menus.
