Hi, I'm Sam 👋

The face (and stomach) behind Eat With Sam K

I've always loved discovering great food. Whether it's a hidden neighborhood spot or the newest restaurant everyone is talking about, I'm always looking for places that are actually worth the visit.

Living in Nashville has given me the chance to explore one of the country's best food scenes, and traveling has shown me just how many incredible restaurants are out there. But I've also learned that finding the right place isn't always easy. With so many options and countless online reviews, it can be difficult to know what's genuinely worth your time and money.

That's why I created Eat With Sam K.

🎯 My mission is simple: provide honest, unbiased restaurant reviews that help people find amazing food.

Every meal you see on this site is paid for with my own money. I don't accept paid reviews, and I never change my opinion in exchange for free food. If I recommend a restaurant, it's because I truly enjoyed the experience and would happily go back.

My goal is to build more than just another food blog. I want Eat With Sam K to become Nashville's most trusted restaurant guide—a place where locals and visitors can discover everything from the city's best burgers and pizza to date-night favorites, hidden gems, brunch spots, and can't-miss local experiences.

As I continue exploring Nashville (and eventually other cities), I'll keep adding new reviews, rankings, neighborhood guides, curated lists, and honest recommendations to make choosing your next meal a little easier.

Thanks for stopping by and supporting Eat With Sam K. I hope this site helps you discover your next favorite restaurant.

How I Rate

Every review is scored consistently, the same criteria whether it's a $4 slice or a $200 tasting menu: taste, value, atmosphere, and service. You'll find the full breakdown on each individual review page. Here's exactly what each score means:

9.8–10.0 🏆 Once-in-a-lifetime. Nearly perfect. Among the best restaurants I've ever experienced.
9.5–9.7 ⭐ Elite. I'd recommend it to anyone. Very few places should reach this level.
9.0–9.4 🔥 Outstanding. One of the best restaurants in its category. I'd happily return again and again.
8.5–8.9 ✅ Excellent. A place I'd confidently recommend and revisit.
8.0–8.4 👍 Really Good. Definitely worth trying, even if it isn't a city-wide standout.
7.5–7.9 🙂 Good. I enjoyed it and wouldn't hesitate to eat there again.
7.0–7.4 😐 Decent. Nothing wrong with it, but I probably wouldn't go out of my way.
6.0–6.9 👎 Below Average. There are better options nearby.
5.0–5.9 ❌ Poor. I wouldn't recommend it.
Below 5.0 🚫 Major disappointment. I would not return.
