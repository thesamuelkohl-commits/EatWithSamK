Ways to Work Together

Four ways brands usually work with me. UGC is the big one, so it has its own section below.

🎥 Sponsored Features

📢 Guide Page Ads

An ad placement on one of my guides, pages people come back to and share long after they're published. Since restaurants are what's being reviewed on those pages, this tends to fit non-restaurant brands (delivery apps, reservation platforms, credit cards, other food-adjacent businesses) better than it fits restaurants.

✨ Something Else in Mind?

Newsletter mentions, event coverage, or anything else, happy to hear the idea and figure out if it's a fit.

📸 UGC Content

UGC (user-generated content) is video and photo I shoot for your brand, that you own and post yourself. It's made to look like a real person recommending you, not a polished commercial, because that's what performs on Instagram, TikTok, and paid social right now.

You get the files. Run them on your own channels, cut them into ads, put them on your website. I don't have to post any of it.

🍽️ What I Shoot

  • Vertical short-form video for Reels, TikTok, Shorts, and paid social
  • Food and product close-ups, menu highlights, reactions, unboxings
  • Restaurant and hospitality atmosphere, drinks, venues, destinations
  • Hooks, concepts, scripts, voiceover, and testable creative angles
  • Social-ready photography, plus raw footage and alternate cuts
