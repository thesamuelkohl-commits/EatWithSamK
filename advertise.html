Every partnership is built around clear deliverables, not a vague "exposure" package. A typical guide placement or sponsored video includes:

  • A dedicated video review, shot and edited in my normal style, posted to Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
  • A featured write-up in the most relevant "Best Of" guide, with a direct link back to you
  • Usage rights to repost the content on your own channels, with credit
  • One round of revisions before anything goes live

Exact scope and turnaround depend on the partnership. Happy to put together a custom package for what you actually need.
