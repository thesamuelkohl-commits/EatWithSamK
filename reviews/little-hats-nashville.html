About Little Hats Italian Market

Little Hats is an Italian market and deli in Germantown, behind a green door on 4th Avenue North. Inside it's shelves of imported Italian goods around a long counter-service dining room, with sandwiches and pasta made to order and patio tables out front in the middle of the neighborhood's shops.

What I Ordered

The Crispy Ravioli, the Meatball Parmesan, and the Bolognese & Rigatoni.
