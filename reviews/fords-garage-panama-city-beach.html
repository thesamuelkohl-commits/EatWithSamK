About Ford's Garage

Ford's Garage is a burger and craft beer restaurant styled like a vintage Ford service station, and this one sits in Pier Park. There's a Model A up on a lift over the bar, a long wall of beer taps behind it, and a patio out front.

What I Ordered

The Giant Funnel Tower of Piston Onion Rings, the Model A burger, and the Patty Melt.
