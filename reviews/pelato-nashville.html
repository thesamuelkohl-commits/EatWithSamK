About Pelato

Pelato is a Brooklyn-Italian, family-run restaurant in Nashville's Germantown neighborhood, serving small, sharable plates built around cherished family recipes — handmade pasta, wood-fired dishes, and a lively bar scene, with 2-3 dishes per person recommended.

What I Ordered

Mozzarella Caprese, two orders of the Radiatori Vodka, Chicken Parmigiana, and the Campanelle Shrimp Limone.
