About Thai Samurai

Thai Samurai serves Thai food and sushi, and this is their River North location on the East Bank. There's a tuk-tuk parked out front, and the bar sits under big backlit samurai murals. The Flaming Tiger Roll comes out wrapped in foil and gets set on fire at the table.

What I Ordered

A Thai Spicy Margarita, the Volcanic Lobster, Drunken Noodles with chicken at level 3 hot, and the Flaming Tiger Roll.
