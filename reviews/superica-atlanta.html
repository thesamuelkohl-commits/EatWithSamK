About Superica

Superica is a Tex-Mex restaurant and cantina at The Battery Atlanta, right next to Truist Park, serving queso, tacos al carbon, fajitas, and margaritas in a lively, baseball-adjacent atmosphere.

What I Ordered

The queso with picadillo and Tacos al Carbon with steak, chicken, and pork.
