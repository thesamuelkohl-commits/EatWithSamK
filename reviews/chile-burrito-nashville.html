About The Chile Burrito Co.

The Chile Burrito Co. is a counter-service Mexican spot in downtown Nashville, just off Broadway, serving build-your-own burritos, tacos, and salsa bar sides in a colorful, casual dining room.

What I Ordered

A 12-inch burrito with steak, rice, cheese, queso, pico, cilantro, onions, lettuce, and medium sauce, plus a side of chips.
