About GoodTimes Full Service Bar

GoodTimes is a full service bar in Wedgewood-Houston, a green building with GOODTIMES in red letters out front and "all day, decent eats, strong drinks" painted across the wall. Inside it's a retro room with red vinyl stools along the bar and a green checkerboard floor, and the kitchen keeps it simple with smash burgers and wings.

What I Ordered

Six buffalo wings, the Fried Cheese Wedges, a Smash Burger, and fries.
