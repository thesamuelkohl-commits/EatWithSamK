About Maru WeHo

Maru is a Nashville-based sushi restaurant group with several locations across the city. This one sits in the Wedgewood-Houston (WeHo) neighborhood just south of downtown, in a sleek, industrial-style space with a full sushi bar, specialty rolls, and a cocktail menu.
