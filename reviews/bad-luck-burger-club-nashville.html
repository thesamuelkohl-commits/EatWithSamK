About Bad Luck Burger Club

Bad Luck Burger Club is a smash burger trailer parked in East Nashville on Woodland Street, covered in yellow lettering and cartoon cats. You order at the window, and there's a bar next door with indoor seating if you'd rather not eat standing up.

What I Ordered

The Bad Luck Burger, the Island Boi Burger, and a side of Tots.
