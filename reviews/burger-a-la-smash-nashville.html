About Burger A La Smash

Burger A La Smash is a counter-service smash burger spot on Murfreesboro Pike, in a repurposed fast food building painted red, white and blue. You order at the counter under the menu boards and take a tray to the dining room or the patio.

What I Ordered

The Black Truffle Burger, the Mac N Fry Chicken, the Hot Nacho Smash Burger, and 10 hot honey wings.
