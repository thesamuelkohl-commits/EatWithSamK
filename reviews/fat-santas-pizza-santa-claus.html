About Fat Santa's Pizza & More

Fat Santa's Pizza & More is a pizza place in Santa Claus, Indiana, a few minutes from Holiday World. It's a barn-style building with a lit-up Santa sign out front, a checkerboard floor and red diner chairs inside, Christmas decorations up year round, and arcade games for the kids.

What I Ordered

The Garlic Cheesy Bread and a 16-inch pizza, half Down The Chimney and half Founders Special.
