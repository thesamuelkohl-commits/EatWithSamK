About Chilangos Tacos

Chilangos Tacos is a taco stand on Level 2 North of Assembly Food Hall at Fifth + Broadway in downtown Nashville, the Nashville outpost of a Dallas-based taco brand serving tacos, quesadillas, and nachos with a mix of classic and specialty fillings.

What I Ordered

A chicken, steak, and pastor (pork) taco, plus a side of chips and queso.
