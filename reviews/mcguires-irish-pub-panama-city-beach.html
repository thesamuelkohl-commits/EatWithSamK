About McGuire's Irish Pub

McGuire's is a 550-seat Irish pub and steakhouse on Front Beach Road, across from the Gulf. It's the third McGuire's, after Pensacola and Destin, and opened in 2025. Signed dollar bills cover the ceiling and every wall, they brew their own ales in house, and Irish musicians play late every night.

What I Ordered

The McGuire's Root Beer, the 18¢ Senate Bean Soup, Reuben Egg Rolls, a Filet Mignon, the Pasta Rustica, and the Garlic Mashed Potatoes.
